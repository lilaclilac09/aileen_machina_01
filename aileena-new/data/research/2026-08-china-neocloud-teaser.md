# Is a Terrestrial Neocloud Possible in China? — Teaser · 概念扫盲

**The megawatts are there. The company is not.** 15 August 2026. Site: `/blog/china-neocloud-teaser`. Companion analysis: `/blog/china-neocloud` (14 August).

This is the method piece, not the hangar tour. Structure: what a neocloud is / how big / what is inside → comparable campuses → how a number is verified (unit + SKU) → power price and usable compute → whose order → what SemiAnalysis watches.

# Takeaway

A neocloud is a company that sells reserved cluster hours to a third party and finances the cards. Keep three tables unmerged: the shell, the power, and the order. A megawatt in the first is not an offtake in the third. That company is not in the taxi on Huawei Avenue.

# Four tests (all required)

Frontier GPUs in training quantities. A fabric of about 10,000 cards. Reserved cluster hours sold to a third party at high ACV. GPUs financed like aircraft. Fail one and the firm is something else. Fail all four and it is a landlord with a liquid-cooling brochure.

# Units

W = one card (H100/H200 ~700 W; B200 ~1,000–1,200 W). kW = one rack (GB200 NVL72 ~120–132 kW). MW = campus / hall group. GW = contracted or active fleet. Do not add a city's 1 GW to a campus door. Do not add contracted GW to active GW. Convert power to card counts only after the SKU is named.

# Power / GPU-hour (mid-2026 prints)

Ulanqab door-to-door ~0.33–0.36 yuan/kWh (~$0.05 at 7.2). China closed H100 lease 75–80k yuan/month (~$14–15/hour). Semi one-year H100 $1.70 (Oct 2025) / $2.35 (Mar 2026). The west is cheap on power and expensive on the closed H100 lease — that gap is the export-control card.

# Whose order (Bayin)

Alibaba = field sighting (Yicai, 13 Aug 2026). DeepSeek = city-level Ulanqab ~1 GW (Bloomberg, 31 Jul 2026), no Bayin door pin. GDS = city MoU, not a Bayin take-or-pay. Do not collapse landlord / tenant / walk-in.
